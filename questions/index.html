HERE TO MAKE IT SIMPLE

A few things
you might be wondering.

Do I send you my photographs?

No. You rent the scanner and use it at home, so your original photographs stay with you throughout.

Is postage included?

Yes, postage is included in all four rental packages. Local collection is also available.

How long can I rent the scanner for?

Choose 4 days for £75, 7 days for £95, 14 days for £135 or 30 days for £225.

Can I collect the scanner locally?

Yes. Choose local collection in the booking journey. Collection location and arrangements will be provided before a booking is confirmed.

How do I book a scanner?

Go to Book a scanner, choose your rental dates, and complete your booking through Booqable.
